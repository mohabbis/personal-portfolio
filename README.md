# muharafiq.com

Personal website and digital portfolio for Muhammad Rafiq.

Built as an evolving collection of projects, systems, experiments, photography, and design work.

**Live Site:** https://www.muharafiq.com

---

## Philosophy

> Leaving every room a little brighter.

Rather than functioning as a traditional resume, the portfolio documents the process of turning ideas into working systems.

Projects span branding, digital experiences, creative technology, operational systems, environmental technology, photography, and web experiences.

The focus is not credentials.

The focus is thoughtful execution.

---

## Featured Areas

### Lumen

A modern smart-home experience built around rooms, scenes, and intent-based automation.

Focused on clarity, automation, environmental awareness, and calm interaction design.

### Modern Branding For Local Businesses

A collection of branding, web, and digital experience work for local businesses.

Includes Fancy Car Wash and future client work.

### Creative Systems

An ongoing exploration of the space between design, software, automation, infrastructure, and physical environments.

Concepts become systems.

Systems become experiences.

---

## Design System

The visual direction is intentionally editorial.

- Warm neutrals
- Brown, tan, and cream foundations
- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Minimal interface chrome
- Photography-forward layouts

The goal is to feel closer to a printed publication than a software dashboard.

---

## Technical Stack

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Vercel

## Future Direction

muharafiq.com is intended to grow into a long-term archive of projects, ideas, and systems.

Over time, the site will expand beyond a portfolio and become a personal studio.

- Case studies
- Design experiments
- Photography collections
- Smart-home research
- Product concepts
- Local business branding work
- Technical infrastructure projects
- Writing and reflections

The goal is to build a body of work that remains useful years from now.

Not a collection of accomplishments.

A collection of systems.

## Maintenance Philosophy

- Prioritize clarity over complexity.
- Remove more than you add.
- Favor systems over features.
- Tell stories through projects.
- Show the work.
- Keep the experience calm, intentional, and timeless.

The best version of the site feels effortless.

Calm, clear, and intentional.

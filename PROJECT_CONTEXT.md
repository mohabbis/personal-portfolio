# PROJECT_CONTEXT.md

## Project Overview

This repository powers Muhammad Rafiq's personal portfolio website.

Primary domain:

- https://www.muharafiq.com

The website should function as a creative portfolio and digital home rather than a traditional resume.

The goal is to showcase:

- Design thinking
- Product thinking
- Web development
- Branding work
- Photography
- Smart-home and technology projects
- Systems thinking
- Creative experimentation

## Core Brand Positioning

Primary line:

> Leaving every room a little brighter.

The site should communicate curiosity, craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and technical execution.

Avoid making the site feel like a recruiting landing page.

## Visual Direction

Style:

- Luxury editorial
- Warm and refined
- Minimal but not sterile
- Premium without appearing corporate

Palette:

- Brown
- Tan
- Cream
- Warm neutrals

Typography direction:

- Instrument Serif
- Raleway
- Geist Mono for metadata and supporting details

Avoid:

- Generic startup aesthetics
- Heavy gradients
- Excessive animations
- Overly technical visual language

## Content Priorities

Order of importance:

1. Projects
2. Creative work
3. Systems and experiments
4. Photography
5. Professional experience

Project storytelling is more important than credentials.

Avoid emphasizing:

- GPA
- Coursework
- Academic achievements
- Major references

Unless explicitly requested.

## User Experience Principles

The website should feel:

- Personal
- Intentional
- Curated
- Calm
- Fast

Visitors should discover work naturally rather than being overwhelmed by information.

Reduce unnecessary navigation complexity.

Favor clarity over density.

## Technical Requirements

Framework:

- Next.js
- TypeScript

Deployment:

- Vercel

Expected qualities:

- Fast load times
- Mobile-first responsiveness
- Accessibility awareness
- Strong SEO fundamentals
- Clean component architecture

## Known Direction

The website should evolve toward a studio/gallery experience.

Long-term categories may include:

- Projects
- Perspectives
- Photography
- Experiments
- Systems
- Materials
- Interfaces
- Fragments

These sections should showcase both finished work and ongoing exploration.

## Success Criteria

A successful visitor should leave understanding:

- What Muhammad builds
- How Muhammad thinks
- The quality standard behind the work
- The range of creative and technical projects being explored

The site should leave a stronger impression of taste, execution, and systems thinking than a conventional resume website.
